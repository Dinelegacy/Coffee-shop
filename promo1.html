American Pancakes

Easy, American-style, fluffy pancakes are great for feeding a crowd at breakfast or brunch. Top with something sweet like fruit, jam or syrup, or rashers of crispy bacon.

What it contains

All of our products can contain small traces of allergens. They are handled with great care, but due to the fact that products with different allergens are handled in the same premises, allergens can be transferred to other products.
